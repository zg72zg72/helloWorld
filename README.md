# FIRE Calculator

A simple, beautiful web calculator to help you plan your journey to Financial Independence and Early Retirement (FIRE).

## Features

- Calculate your FIRE number based on annual expenses
- Estimate years until financial independence
- Track your current progress toward FIRE
- Visualize your path with an interactive progress bar
- Customizable withdrawal rate and expected returns

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Enter your financial information:
   - **Annual Expenses**: How much you spend per year
   - **Current Savings**: Your total savings and investments
   - **Annual Savings**: How much you can save each year
   - **Expected Annual Return**: Your expected investment returns (typically 5-8%)
   - **Safe Withdrawal Rate**: The 4% rule is commonly used
3. Click "Calculate FIRE Number" to see your results

## The 4% Rule

The calculator uses the traditional FIRE formula based on the 4% rule, which suggests you can safely withdraw 4% of your portfolio annually in retirement. Your FIRE number is calculated as:

**FIRE Number = Annual Expenses ÷ Safe Withdrawal Rate**
